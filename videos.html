Burlington City Arts Artist Talk: Hyunsuk Erickson on "Thingumabob Society"

Sep 21, 2023

"Thingumabob Society" artist Hyunsuk Erickson, joined BCA for an interactive virtual discussion on September 7, 2023. Erickson shared her fascinating process that merges sculpture and craft traditions and the compelling stories behind her vibrant installation, which was on view at the BCA Center from May 19-September 17, 2023.
