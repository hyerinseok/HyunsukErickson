“Entangled Worlds” @ Sandy Spring Museum

May 3 – August 5, 2026 @ Sandy Spring Museum in Sandy Spring, MD

“Entangled Worlds: Thingumabobs and the Duality of Identity
“Entangled Worlds” by Hyunsuk Erickson blends natural and industrial materials to create hybrid forms that are soft, resilient, and alive with memory. The work invites reflection on care, connection, and coexistence, showing how human, nonhuman, and speculative bodies persist together in fragile, intertwined systems.
